## What the test is

Navy PRT, three events:

- **Push-ups**, 2 minutes, max reps
- **Forearm plank**, max hold
- **1.5-mile run**, timed

Alternate cardio events exist at the CO's discretion (2000 m row, 500 yd swim,
12-minute bike). Curl-ups have been prohibited since NAVADMIN 024/21. Sit-ups
are still on the NSW PST, which is a different test, so do not let the two
blur together.

**That event list is recall, not a sourced citation.** It matches what I
believe the current PRT to be, and nothing here depends on it being exactly
right, but confirm it against the live guidance before you plan around it. It
came out of the same channel as the scoring tables below, and I am flagging it
the same way rather than treating one as fact and the other as suspect.

**Scoring tables are deliberately not in this document.** Your own research
notes mark them UNVERIFIED with an explicit instruction not to fill them in
from memory, and that instruction binds here. If you want to know what score a
given time or rep count earns, that is a sourced-research task, not something
to recall.

## The delivery problem, stated plainly

Your cardio program is Garmin's, not OptiGains'.

The activity names in `garmin_activities` are Garmin's daily-suggested-workout
labels with a location prefix ("Laramie - Threshold", "Natrona County - VO2
Max", "Laramie - Zone 2"). You are following Garmin's recommendations, which
is what you said and the data confirms.

That means writing PRT run prescriptions into OptiGains puts them somewhere you
do not look. Prescribing you a threshold run competes with Garmin for the same
session and you will follow Garmin.

**So the running needs nothing from OptiGains.** It is already handled, by a
system you actually follow, and it is producing a defensible 1.5-mile.

What Garmin never prescribes is push-ups and plank. That is the gap, and it is
the only place OptiGains adds anything for this test.

## The three things worth doing

### 1. Run a timed 1.5-mile and log it in the PST card

This is the highest-leverage single action available, and not for the reason
I would have given a week ago.

It is not a fitness probe. You have a 4 to 5 day per week base, so a timed 1.5
is a rehearsal, not a discovery. The reason to do it is that it is the one
input that unlocks pace prescriptions across the entire engine:

```
pst_tests.run_seconds set
  -> vdot_engine.record_effort(2414.0, seconds)
  -> self.validated = True
  -> generate_weekly_program.py:613-616 stops withholding pace
```

Right now every prescribed run in OptiGains carries `pace: null`, and it will
keep doing that forever until a real timed effort exists. The engine already
prints the remedy in its own output: "log a timed 1.5-mile to unlock pace
targets."

The entry point exists and works: the **PST Performance** card, "Log Test"
button, at `/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/src/components/PSTTracker.jsx`. Fill
the run minutes and seconds. It also takes push-ups and plank, so one submit
covers all three baselines.

Do it early in the window, not the day before the test. If it lands Saturday
Sep 12 or Sunday Sep 13, you get a real number and still have a full week.

### 2. Push-ups and plank, into the cardio block

Two exposures a week, kept deliberately small. This is skill and pacing work
for a 2-minute max, not a hypertrophy stimulus:

- **Push-ups:** 3 sets, stopping 3 to 5 reps short of failure, 90 seconds rest.
  The point is rehearsing the 2-minute pacing, not accumulating fatigue.
- **Plank:** 2 holds at roughly 60 percent of your best hold, which you will
  know once you log one.

Honest caveat on where this can go: there is no surface in the app that both
renders push-ups **and** lives in the cardio column.

- `program_workouts.cardio_sessions` is literally the cardio column and is
  separate from `exercises`, so writing there cannot touch your lift.
- It does render, in WorkoutDetail's `CardioSessions`
  (`/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/src/pages/WorkoutDetail.jsx:55-92`), and a
  non-run entry neither blanks nor crashes it. But every field that component
  reads is run-shaped. The label falls through
  `run_type` to `zone` to the literal string `"Run"`
  (`/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/src/pages/WorkoutDetail.jsx:68`), so a
  push-up entry renders titled **"Run"** unless I write a run-shaped field to
  steer it. The meta line offers minutes, pace, zone and time-of-day
  (`:70-75`) and has no slot for sets or reps, so the prescription itself has
  to live in free text in `notes`.
- The dashboard's PrescribedSessionCard renders only `run_block`,
  `swim_block` and `calisthenics_block`, and it is hardcoded to those three
  (`/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/src/components/dashboard/PrescribedSessionCard.jsx:384`).
  A push-up block written into `cardio_sessions` will not appear on Today.

So the honest position: I can write it, and it will show up correctly enough
if I steer the label, but only on a screen you have to go looking for, with
the actual sets and reps as a sentence rather than as structure. For two weeks
that is worse than a note on your phone. Take the schedule from this document,
and let the app's inability to hold it be a UX finding rather than something I
work around in your training data.

### 3. A cardio-only taper, last four to five days

Drop cardio volume, keep frequency and a little intensity. Lifting stays
exactly as programmed. Since Garmin drives your running, this mostly means
overriding Garmin's suggestion for a few days rather than anything OptiGains
does.

## What I changed in the code

**Push-ups no longer count as hypertrophy volume.**
`/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/scripts/engine/muscle_map.py`

`get_muscle_credit()` now returns `{}` for any push-up variant. Everything
else about push-ups is unchanged: `get_muscles()` and `hypertrophy_muscles()`
still report chest and triceps, so deviation tracking, session pattern dedup,
the caution/pain matcher and the landmark response slopes all still know a
push-up is a pressing movement. Only the weighted volume counter is exempt.

Why it needed a code change rather than a note:

- The volume path is live. Those four June push-up logs each credited chest
  1.0 and triceps 0.5.
- The counter does not look at reps. `compute_athlete_state.py:339` computes
  `hard_sets` as the number of sets with a weight **or** a rep value, so a
  1-rep push-up entry counted as a full hard set, identical to a real working
  set of twelve.
- It is structural, not just stray manual logs. At
  `session_generator.py:2503`, push-ups are routed into `calisthenics_block`
  only when the selection is unpinned. When a plan owns the session they stay
  in `strength_block`, get logged as exercises, and land right back in the
  volume counter. Programming push-ups for the PRT without this exemption
  would have suppressed your prescribed pressing sets.

Substring match, so "Weighted Push-up" is exempt too. That is the plain
reading of "don't count the push-ups as volume" rather than a carve-out you
did not ask for. Say the word if you want weighted variants counted.

Pinned with two assertions in
`/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/scripts/validate_convergence_fixes.py`, next
to the existing guard, so a future refactor cannot collapse the two paths back
together. All 225 checks pass.

**Live since 2026-09-10** (commit `8d6f6056`). The cron picked it up on the next
nightly run.

**Plank joined the exemption**, and a PRT prep block now rides the conditioning
days. `VOLUME_EXEMPT_KEYWORDS` is `("push up", "pushup", "plank")`, and
`_add_prt_block` in
`/home/nolan/projects/OptiGains/scripts/engine/session_generator.py` appends
Push-ups (3 x 20-25, submaximal) and Plank (3 x 60s) to any day that carries a
run, between 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-27. Outside that window it does nothing, so
the block expires on its own instead of becoming a permanent rule.

The lift is untouched: on the same date the strength selection is identical with
the block on and off, because the injection runs after the knapsack has already
chosen the session and neither movement counts toward hypertrophy volume.

The rule behind the exemption is that a scored PRT event is not hypertrophy
volume. Push-ups and the forearm plank are two of the three things the test
measures, they are scored in reps and seconds, and neither is a set taken near
failure. Your call, 2026-09-10. Settled, not open.
