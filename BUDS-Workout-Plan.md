# 12-Week BUD/S Preparation Plan
*Target: Ready for BUD/S by end of August*

This plan is modeled after Stew Smith's "12 Weeks to BUD/S" from *The Complete Guide to Navy SEAL Fitness*. It focuses on building the extreme muscle stamina and cardiovascular endurance required to not just pass, but compete at BUD/S.

## The Standard: Competitive PST Scores
Aim for these numbers by Week 12:
- **500 Yard Swim:** < 9:00 (Combat Side Stroke or Breaststroke)
- **Pushups:** 100+ in 2:00
- **Situps:** 100+ in 2:00
- **Pullups:** 20+
- **1.5 Mile Run:** < 9:00 (in running shoes) / < 10:30 (in boots/pants)

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
**Focus:** Technique, baseline volume, and establishing a running/swimming base.

### Weekly Schedule
- **Mon:** PST Baseline (Week 1) or Upper Body Pyramid (1-10-1)
- **Tue:** LSD Run (3-4 miles) + LSD Swim (1000m)
- **Wed:** Upper Body Super Sets (Push/Pull/Abs) + Hypoxic Swim
- **Thu:** Lower Body PT (Squats/Lunges/Sprints) + LSD Swim (1000m)
- **Fri:** Timed Run (4 miles) + Sprints
- **Sat:** Upper Body PT (Max Reps) + Long Swim (1500m CSS)
- **Sun:** Rest

---

## Phase 2: Stamina & Intensity (Weeks 5-8)
**Focus:** Increasing volume, "600 Pushup" days, and Run-Swim-Run combinations.

### Weekly Schedule
- **Mon:** The "600 Pushup" Super Set (20 cycles of 30 pushups/20 abs)
- **Tue:** Run-Swim-Run (3mi Run / 1mi Swim / 3mi Run)
- **Wed:** Upper Body Pyramid (1-12-1) + Hypoxic Pyramid (2, 4, 6, 8, 10 strokes)
- **Thu:** Lower Body PT (Heavy Lunges/Squats) + 2000m Swim with Fins
- **Fri:** 5-mile Timed Run
- **Sat:** Upper Body Super Sets + 1-mile Swim (No Fins)
- **Sun:** Rest

---

## Phase 3: Peak & Endurance (Weeks 9-12)
**Focus:** Maximum volume, boots/pants running, and mental toughness.

### Weekly Schedule
- **Mon:** Max Reps / PST Check (Every 2nd week)
- **Tue:** 6-mile LSD Run + 2000m Swim with Fins
- **Wed:** Upper Body "Grinder PT" (30 min non-stop calisthenics) + 10x100m Swim Sprints
- **Thu:** 4-mile Run in Boots/Pants + Lower Body Plyometrics
- **Fri:** Timed Run (5-6 miles)
- **Sat:** Long Day (90-120 min mixed cardio: Run/Swim/Calisthenics)
- **Sun:** Rest

---

## Core Workout Details

### 1. Upper Body Pyramid (1-10-1)
Do 1 rep of each, then 2, then 3... up to 10, then back down to 1.
- Pullups (x1)
- Pushups (x2)
- Situps (x3)
- Dips (x2)
*Total: 100 Pullups, 200 Pushups, 300 Situps, 200 Dips.*

### 2. Hypoxic Swim Pyramid
Swim freestyle. Breathe every X strokes.
- 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 strokes per breath (by 50m/100m segments).
*Note: Never do hypoxic training alone.*

### 3. Lower Body PT
- Squats: 3 sets of 50
- Lunges: 3 sets of 20 per leg
- Calf Raises: 3 sets of 50
- 1/4 mile Sprints: 4-6 sets (rest 1:1)

### 4. 8-Count Body Builders
1. Squat down, hands on floor.
2. Kick feet back to pushup position.
3. Drop to chest (bottom of pushup).
4. Push back up.
5. Kick feet out (into jumping jack/plank position).
6. Pull feet back together.
7. Pull feet back to squat position.
8. Stand up/Jump.
