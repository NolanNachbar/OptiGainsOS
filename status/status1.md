## 8. Related Projects / Market Research
- What projects are similar or already doing what your project is doing?  
- How is your project different from existing ones?  
- *(If necessary, note how this may require updates to Section 5 above.)*

There is an app called MacroFactor, which does everything we want it to, but it costs quite a bit of money to use. It also has it connect with the health app on your phone to track everything.
It does not generate workouts for but it does track how much you should you eat and calorie intake for your goal weight or body fat. The majority of the workout "generator" apps on the market
are mostly pre-made workouts and nothing that can be truly adjusted without some knowledge and experience. Which is something we want ours to do automatically, especially for newcomers. 

Similar existing workout apps include:
- **Fitbod** — for automated workout suggestions.  
- **Strong** — for logging workouts manually.
- **Fitness AI** - AI-powered workout planner that generates personalized strength training routines based on user input.


We found that existing lifting apps focus on workout-to-workout tracking, logging sets, reps, and weights, and suggesting load progressions. However, they rarely account for user enjoyment, cumulative fatigue, soreness, or CNS recovery, and they take a more workout-to-workout or lift-to-lift approach instead of focusing on long-term progressions.


Our app is different by adapting to individual recovery and fatigue, incorporating both short-term and long-term progress into the recommendations, allowing personalized exercise swaps, and combining workout tracking with macro tracking.
