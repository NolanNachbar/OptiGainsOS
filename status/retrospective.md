# Senior Design 1 Recommendations

## What went well and should be continued?
Having classes where we go over what our status is on the project, improving our public speaking skills, and confidence. 

Allowing teams to pick projects that they are genuinely interested in made it easier to stay motivated. Also, exploring the use of AI tools was useful because in the real world, we'll probably have to learn to use them or may get left behind.

Periodic updates and planning documents were helpful to keep us on track.

## What didn't go so well and should be changed?

Clarify expectations and requirements for assignments. Possibly offer refreshers on diagrams and layouts since many of us haven’t done them in a while.

Encourage Juniors to think about what they want their project to be so they're prepared.

When the groups get together and present what they want their project to be, don't expect them to have all the details figured out.
