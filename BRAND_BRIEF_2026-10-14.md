# Brand brief (draft for decision) — 2026-10-14

Basis: `BRAND_UX_AUDIT_2026-10-14.md`. Assumption confirmed by Nolan: brand as if
public. Method follows brand-identity practice (meaning → name → system → mark);
it is not an imitation of any individual designer's work.

## Position
**The training instrument that shows its working.** Every number carries its
reason; nothing is performed at you. This is true of the product today (a
deterministic engine with traceable output) and is a position generic apps
cannot copy without rebuilding.

## Principles
1. Show the working. 2. One flat, exact voice. 3. Restraint is the finish.
(Detail in the audit §2.)

## Who it is for (public)
Self-coached lifters who want load, volume and nutrition decisions explained,
not scored. Not for people who want motivation theatre.

## Name — open decision
Current: OptiGains / OptiGainsOS / OPTIGAINS (four spellings in the product).
Concerns for a public name: "Opti-" prefix is crowded in the App Store (Opti Train,
OptiFit, OptiLife); "Gains" is generic; "OS" adds nothing. No exact "OptiGains"
app found, but descriptive names are weak to defend.

Candidates that express "shows its working" (preliminary web checks only — NOT a
trademark or domain search; each needs one before commitment):

| Candidate | Why | Collision notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Basis** | Engine "shows its basis"; pairs with the audit's "Basis:" line | Old Basis wearable brand (2013–16); a gym-booking app "Fitness Basis". Very common word → hard to own/search |
| **Reckon** | To calculate; colloquial; one word | No iOS app by name found; adjacent: "Reckoner", a Garmin strength-progression app (same job) |
| **Workings** | "Show your workings" | "Working Sets" and "Worked" exist in the App Store |
| ~~Tally~~ | — | Taken: Tally Workout Tracker (strength, Claude MCP) |
| Keep **OptiGains** | Zero migration cost | Descriptive, crowded prefix |

Recommendation: decide between **keeping OptiGains** (cheap, defensible only
with strong distinctive identity) or a coined/evocative name after a real
trademark + domain + handle search. Do not ship a rename on this evidence.

## Mark territories (concepts in `brand/concepts/`)
- **A · Gauge** — 270° instrument arc, butt caps, one reading tick. Evolves the
  current ring; at small size risks "generic progress ring".
- **B · Working** — three ruled lines of a calculation; the last, full-weight bar
  is the answer. Strongest idea (it *is* the position); needs a refinement pass
  so it does not read as a hamburger/menu glyph at 29px.
- **C · Basis** — a point on a measured scale. Reads closest to a barbell/person
  glyph in the first render; weakest. Keep only as a fallback.
All use the system's own palette: `#0B0C0E` field, `#ECEEF1` mark. No gradient,
no brand hue (teal reserved for "readiness" data).
