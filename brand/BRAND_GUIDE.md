# OptiGains

**The training instrument that shows its working.**

OptiGains prescribes loads, volume and food from a deterministic engine, and every
number it gives you carries the reason it was chosen. Other apps score you. This one
shows the arithmetic.

This is the brand book. Read it in this order:

1. **README** (this page): what the brand is, who it is for, the rules.
2. **Identity options**: the directions considered. Option 2, The Instrument, is chosen and live.
3. **Voice**: how OptiGains writes, with before and after examples from the app.
4. **Foundations**: colour law, type, motion, data, iconography.

## What it is

A private-first training and nutrition engine for self-coached lifters. It runs a
readiness model, a strength progression, and a food plan from your own logged data.
It is not a motivation product. There is no streak shaming, no confetti, no score you
cannot trace.

## Who it is for

The lifter who keeps a spreadsheet because no app explains itself. They want to know
why 242.5 and not 245, why today is a hold, why the target is what it is. They log
honestly and read the numbers.

## Position

OptiGains is the training instrument that shows its working. Every engine number has
a basis one tap away. Nothing is performed at you.

The position is true of the product today. The engine is deterministic Python with
traceable output, and the interface can say so without exaggeration. Competitors built
on opaque scores cannot copy this without rebuilding.

## Three principles

1. **Show the working.** Any number the engine sets or changes carries its basis in
   one line: "242.5 lb: top set 8 reps at 2 RIR last week, +5 lb step." Reasoning is
   the luxury. Animation is not.
2. **One voice, flat and exact.** Declarative. Past tense for logs. Units always
   present. No exclamation marks, no "successfully", no cheering.
3. **Restraint is the finish.** One action colour. Hue means a datum. Every size,
   radius and weight comes from a short named scale. If something needs a new colour,
   size or icon, it is probably the wrong thing.

## The name

**OptiGains.** One word, capital O and G, no space, no hyphen.

- Not "OptiGainsOS". "OS" is a tagline word at most ("a personal performance system"),
  never part of the name.
- Not "OPTIGAINS" in body copy. The all-caps treatment belongs to the wordmark artwork
  only (Option 1), never typed.
- Not "Opti Gains", "Optigains", "OG". Handles and domains may be lowercase.
- Say it as three syllables, OP-ti-GAINS.

Why keep it: it is already the product's name, it is short, and "gains" is what the
user is there for. The cost is that both halves are common words, so the identity has
to do the work the name cannot. That is the job of the mark and the voice.

Open before a public launch: a real trademark, domain and App Store search. Preliminary
web checks found no exact-name app, but the "Opti-" prefix is crowded in fitness
(Opti Train, OptiFit, OptiLife). Treat that as a risk to clear, not a result.

## Tone in one paragraph

Like a lab notebook that happens to be kind. It says what happened, what it means and
what changes, in that order, and stops. It does not apologise, hype, or pad.

## Never

- A gradient, a glow, or a second brand colour.
- Confetti, fireworks, streak counters, "crushed it".
- A hue used for decoration. A hue is a datum.
- A mascot, a human figure, an emoji in product strings.
- A claim the engine cannot back with a number.

# Voice

OptiGains writes like a lab notebook that happens to be kind.

## Rules

1. **Say what happened, then what it means, then what changes.** Stop there.
2. **Past tense for logs.** "Logged 5 sets, 1,080 lb volume." Not "Logged successfully!"
3. **No exclamation marks.** None. Not in toasts, not in empty states.
4. **No "successfully", "oops", "awesome", "crushed".** If it worked, the object and one
   fact are enough.
5. **Units always.** "242.5 lb", "1,800 kcal", "2 RIR". A bare number is a bug.
6. **Name the object.** "Template saved" beats "Saved". "Food entry deleted" beats "Done".
7. **Errors say what failed and what to do.** Not "Failed to save". Say what could not
   be saved and what to try.
8. **Engine output carries its basis.** One line, same shape every time.
9. **The user is never scolded.** A missed session is a fact on a calendar, not a mood.

## Rewrites from the current app

| Today | OptiGains |
| --- | --- |
| Workout logged successfully! | Workout logged. 5 sets, 1,080 lb volume. |
| Food logged successfully! | Logged 6 oz chicken breast to Dinner. |
| Program completed! Congratulations! | Program complete. 12 weeks, 36 sessions, 31 logged. |
| Template saved! | Template saved. 4 items. |
| Profile saved! | Profile saved. |
| Stats saved and goals calculated! | Stats saved. Targets updated: 2,450 kcal, 190 g protein. |
| Nice work. | Check-in saved. Weight 183.4 lb, 7-day avg 183.9. |
| Logged ✓ | Logged 400 kcal to Lunch. |
| Failed to save | Could not save the entry. Check your connection and try again. |
| Failed to log weight | Weight not saved. Try again; nothing was changed. |
| Deload due | Trending down. e1RM is down 3 sessions in a row. |

## The "Basis" line

Wherever the engine sets a value, a single line sits under it. The shape is fixed:

> **Basis:** top set 8 reps at 2 RIR last week, +5 lb step.

> **Basis:** logging covers 6 of 7 days; target uses the 7-day weight trend.

> **Basis:** readiness 71, ACWR 1.12 (sweet spot). Volume held.

Only fields the engine actually emitted may appear. If the engine did not produce the
reason, show nothing. Never invent a reason to fill the line.

## Words we use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| log, logged | track, tracked (as a verb for the athlete) |
| session | workout, when the app is the subject; either is fine in the athlete's own words |
| target, hold, step | goal crushing, level up |
| trending down | deload due, overreached, burnt out |
| readiness | score, points |
| engine | AI, smart, magic |

## Empty states

One sentence that names what will appear and how it gets there. No illustration, no
encouragement.

> No sessions logged yet. Start one from Train and it will appear here.

## Notifications

Title is the object. Body is one fact. No emoji.

> **Today: Upper B** 5 exercises, about 55 min. Top set 187.5 lb.

# Foundations

## Colour law

OptiGains has no brand hue. The identity is form, carried by off-white on a near-black
field. Colour appears in only two ways.

1. **Off-white is the one action colour.** `brand` fills the primary button, the active
   state and the mark. `action-ink` sits on it.
2. **A hue is a datum.** Each hue owns one thing and appears wherever that thing does:
   teal for readiness, coral for protein and resting heart rate, violet for sleep and
   fatigue, green for done and gain, blue for carbs and cardio, yellow for fat.
   Calories are the hero number and take no hue.

The warn and bad hues belong to the biometric spectrum. `bad` is also the single colour
allowed on a destructive control. Anything else that needs attention (a required
field, a soft warning) uses neutral glass and words, never the spectrum.

Light theme exists in the app but fails contrast at its text tiers. Treat the dark
field as the brand and the light theme as a to-do: muted text needs about 66% alpha
and faint text about 52% to clear 4.5:1.

## Type

One family, the platform sans (SF Pro on Apple). No custom face is shipped, which
keeps the app fast and native.

- Weights: 400 for text, 600 for emphasis and labels, 700 for numerals and display.
  Nothing heavier. At present the app spends 700 and 800 about 377 times; collapsing
  them to 600 gives the quieter, more expensive texture the brand wants.
- Numerals are always tabular. Figures that align are a core part of the look.
- Scale: seven steps (11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 28, 44). The app currently uses 25 sizes;
  collapse to these. Nothing below 11px.
- Uppercase labels: 11px, 600, tracked 0.08em, `ink-muted`.

## Form

- Modules are flat full-bleed surfaces separated by a 1px `border` rule. No radius, no
  shadow, no nesting.
- Controls use `radius-control` (10px). Chips and toggles use `radius-pill`.
- One button geometry: 44px tall, 20px side padding, 15px 600 label. Primary is `brand`
  fill. Secondary is a 1px outline. Never icon-only for the primary action.
- Every band gets 16px sides and 12px vertical padding, with a single rule between.

## Motion

- One easing everywhere: `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .3, 1)`.
- 180 to 320ms. Entrances rise 8px. Nothing moves without communicating state.
- No loops except a loading shimmer on `track`. No confetti. No celebration animation,
  now or later: a verified PR is shown as a row in a table.
- Respect reduced motion.

## Data

- Chart marks use the datum's own hue. Axes and gridlines use `border`.
- Label points; no floating unlabelled dots.
- Empty is quiet: a 2px baseline tick in `track`, no zero, no dashed box. When nothing
  is consumed yet, show the target as the value and hide the empty bars.
- The "empty" material is `track`. Skeletons use it too, never a raised surface.

## Iconography

Line icons at one stroke weight, 22px in the dock. No filled icons, no duotone, no
emoji. Two tabs may not share a name (the app has two called "Body" today; one becomes
"State").

## Applications

| Surface | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| App icon | The mark centred on `bg`, full bleed, no rounded corners (the OS masks it). Inside the 80% safe zone. |
| Manifest | `background_color` and `theme_color` are `#0B0C0E`, same as the field. |
| Notification | Title is the object, body is one fact. Badge is the alpha-only silhouette. |
| Splash | The mark, centred. Rises 8px on load. Nothing else. |
| Store listing | Lead with the position, then three screenshots that each show a number and its basis. |
| Marketing | Off-white on near-black. Figures set large and tabular. No people, no photography of gyms. |
